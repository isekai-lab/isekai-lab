# Background visual rubric v1 (frozen for loop 01)

Evaluate visible background art only. Ignore characters, battle UI, text, watermarks and effects belonging to attacks in references. Original assets and layouts are required; similarity of copied objects is not a goal. References anchor artistic principles, not exact scene contents. Town reference: layered waterfront masonry; forest reference: woodland village with a legible passage; cave reference: battle arena rock strata and warm/cool light, NOT an exploration-layout target. Landscape and portrait are judged for their own visible framing; do not penalize portrait for showing less world.

Every criterion is an ordinal integer 0–4. Shared anchors: 0 = absent/unusable or severely broken; 1 = recognizable prototype with conspicuous problems; 2 = coherent and usable but sparse, repetitive or weakly integrated; 3 = convincing, intentionally composed reference-informed result with only localized weaknesses; 4 = unusually strong, cohesive finished art approaching the reference principles. A 4 requires visible evidence; extra objects, stronger bloom, darkness or texture noise do not automatically improve a score. If uncertain between levels choose the lower level and lower confidence.

1. **compositionDepth**: foreground/middle/background separation; asymmetry and focal hierarchy; meaningful terrain height and silhouettes. 1: disconnected primitives or flat stage; 2: depth exists but repeated props/empty gaps dominate; 3: clear framing, coherent connected layers and focal destination; 4: exceptionally controlled rhythm, occlusion and depth throughout.
2. **materialPixels**: stable, deliberately sized pixel detail, material separation and variation. 1: broad flat primitives or distracting noise; 2: materials recognizable but repetitive/coarse at this camera scale; 3: stone/wood/roof/foliage/rock convey purposeful texture at compatible detail scales; 4: rich cohesive detail without noisy or blurry focal surfaces. Do not reward a pixelated final screen by itself.
3. **lightingContact**: readable form, grounded objects, directionally coherent light/shadow, controlled warm/cool hierarchy and atmosphere. 1: floating objects, blown out highlights or unreadable black regions; 2: consistent light but weak contact or excessive flatness; 3: convincing contacts, restrained highlights and layered atmosphere; 4: exceptionally nuanced, legible illumination. Reference blur is optional; readable in-focus play space matters more.
4. **routeReadability**: visible walkable band, entrances, stairs, bridges, path-edge continuity and foreground occlusion. Score only visible affordances, not invisible collision behavior. 1: route hidden/disconnected or staircase visually impossible; 2: route readable with ambiguous edges/obstructions; 3: continuous intuitive passage and destination; 4: clear route plus elegant exploration cues. Do not impose a field-map layout from the cave battle reference.
5. **sceneIdentity**: authored fantasy place rather than generic random props. Town: coherent inhabited architecture and masonry; forest: organic layered woodland and place-specific landmark; cave: connected enclosing rock mass and mineral/water/torch accents. 1: primitive shapes dominate; 2: recognizable setting with sparse generic motifs; 3: cohesive distinctive place with believable scale relationships; 4: memorable coherent environment at reference-level intentionality.

Blocking defects are visible problems that make the scene unusable: missing scene, major floating structures, obscured main route, large clipping holes, severe overexposure/blackout. Ordinary quality shortcomings belong in evidence and nextChanges instead.

Output evidence must name visible locations/objects for each score and actionable next changes. A single model run is subjective and noisy. Do not infer an improvement from attachment order, case IDs, content hashes or a claimed version.
